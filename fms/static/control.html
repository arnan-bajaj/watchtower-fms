Shift match times

To change one match, edit its time in All matches below. Shifting: that match and every later unplayed match get new times, one cycle apart. Teams and results don't change. If TBA is on, the new times are sent there too.

Add a match

Re-adds a qual (e.g. one deleted by mistake) at that number. Put * after a surrogate. Leave Auto started at blank to schedule it to play. If it was already played, enter when auto really started (event-day clock): it opens in review, recounted from the fuel still stored for that time. Then set the hub order, climbs, fouls and any count fixes, and Commit.. Then type its scores and fouls, and Commit.

Cameras

Before you start: mount one camera per hub, fixed rigidly, with a clear view of balls dropping into the hub's opening, and light the field evenly. Aim for 60 fps. Plug the cameras into this computer (USB), or use a network camera. The counter is built in: nothing to install, no model.
Then: press Find cameras and give each hub its camera. Each hub's card below then walks you through the rest a step at a time: picture, fuel colour, Measure, outline, Calibrate, and testing it. Steps appear once the step they need is done.

Event

UTC offset on event day: PDT −7, MDT −6, CDT −5, EDT −4. Used for TBA match times. Changes apply at once; no restart.

Field control (bioarena)

Runs the field from the Match tab: Start starts the field, and the match clock follows it. On the field, open Settings → Watchtower Remote Control, enter this computer's address (below), and copy its key here. These settings stay on this computer.
Turn it off to run matches without the field (Start then only starts the clock here).

Field system (bioarena)

Sends every ball the cameras count straight to the field over UDP, so it decides the AUTO winner and lights the hubs. Leave empty if your field doesn't take counts.

Vision & calibration

Counts per ball: if one ball counts as 8, set 8 and each hub's count is divided by it. Or press Calibrate, drop some balls in that hub, and enter how many. Cameras and outlines: Cameras at the top of Setup.

PINs

4–12 digits; Control's must differ from the others. Changing a PIN logs out every device using it. Scorers (/score) count live; leave theirs blank to turn scorer logins off.

Phone pages

Phones must be on the same WiFi as this computer. Open the page, then Share → Add to Home Screen.

Field sides

Screen order only, on every page. Scores, data and TBA don't change. Any page's ⇄ Flip sides mirrors just that device on top of this.

The Blue Alliance

Keys: sign in at thebluealliance.com → Request write access (thebluealliance.com/request/apiwrite) for your event key. TBA emails an Auth ID and secret per event.
Removes the schedule and every result from the TBA event page. Nothing here is deleted. Send schedule to TBA puts it back. To remove one match, use the TBA column in Schedule → All matches.

Match13

A prediction for the current match, in small grey text after its time on the Match tab. Only this page shows it: never the field TV, the overlay or the phones. Hover over it for the details. Needs internet; without it, nothing shows.
Make a key at match13.com → your account page. It stays on this computer. Match13 is an independent stats site; it predicts from the season's results, not anything at this event.

Match videos

Match videos are links into your YouTube livestream at the right timestamp. Click the button the moment the stream goes live.

Wipe data

A backup of the database is saved to data/backups/ first. Anything already sent to TBA stays on TBA. Wiping deletes it here only.
Wipe playoffs removes alliances, alliance selection and playoff matches, and keeps quals. Wipe everything removes the schedule, all results, fuel, fouls, the TBA queue, the webcast and the field sides. Setup settings (event, teams, TBA, PINs, field, calibration) and logins stay.
